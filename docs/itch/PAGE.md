## Description (paste into the description editor)

---

**PhantomFloor turns a projector and an Android phone into an interactive floor.** Point the
projector at the ground and everything it shows reacts to people: ghostly fog parts around their
feet, spiders scatter from their shoes, snow keeps their footprints, and flowers grow where they
walk.

Nothing is a video loop. Every frame is generated live on your phone's GPU, so it never repeats
and always responds.

### 🎃 Halloween
- **Ectoplasmic Fog**: thick, swirling green-and-purple fog that clears around feet and curls in people's wake.
- **Ground Smoke**: a real fluid simulation of fog-machine smoke that pours in from the edges and flows around legs.
- **The Swarm**: hundreds of spiders that flee from feet, spread panic through the swarm, and scurry out of a sweeping flashlight beam.
- **Cursed Fluid**: a bubbling pool of blood or lava that creeps toward guests and ripples under every step.
- **Living Water**: dark water that rises into tentacles, grasping hands and screaming faces near the people standing on it.

### ❄️ Winter
- **Snowfall**, **Christmas** (twinkling string lights) and **Hanukkah** (blue-and-white lights and a menorah lit for the night you choose). Falling snow settles into a snowy floor that keeps footprints and slowly refills them.

### 🍂 Fall · 🌷 Spring · 🎆 4th of July · ☀️ Summer
- **Fall Leaves**: kick through a carpet of maple and oak leaves that flutter on the wind.
- **Spring Garden**: grass and flowers sprout and bloom wherever people walk.
- **Fireworks**: a firework bursts under every step.
- **Fireflies**: lightning bugs follow you around and slowly blink in sync.
- **Pool Splash**: a sunny pool that ripples as you wade and splashes when you jump in.

### How it reacts
- **Camera tracking:** point the phone's camera at the floor. On-device AI finds feet, or whole people in groups.
- **Sound:** thunder, screams or music make the scenes pulse and flash.
- **Touch:** no camera needed. Your fingers on the phone act as feet.

### What you need
- An Android phone or tablet running Android 8.0 or newer.
- A projector or TV-projector pointed at the floor, connected by **screen mirroring / Miracast**, an
  Android TV device that accepts casting, or an **HDMI / USB-C adapter**. The **Cast** button sends
  the visuals to the projector and keeps the controls on your phone. (Chromecast isn't supported.)
- A dark room. Black means "no light", so the effects look like they're floating on the floor.

### Setup in 2 minutes
1. Install the APK. Android will ask you to allow installs from your browser or file manager.
2. Tap anywhere to open the controls, then press **Cast to projector / TV**.
3. Press **Edit keystone** and drag the 4 corners to fit your floor.
4. Optional: press **Calibrate camera to floor** so tracking lines up with the projection.
5. Pick a season and a scene, and invite people to walk on it.

### Privacy
Camera and microphone are processed **only on your phone**: nothing is recorded or uploaded. The
app uses the internet only to check for a newer version.

### Support
PhantomFloor is free. If it made your party, a small donation keeps new scenes coming. ♥

## Install instructions (paste under the download, or as a section)

1. Download the `.apk` on your Android phone.
2. Open it. If Android asks, allow installs from your browser or file manager.
3. If you see "Blocked by Play Protect", tap **More details → Install anyway**. It's flagged only
   because it doesn't come from the Play Store.
4. Updates from this page install over the old version and keep your settings.
